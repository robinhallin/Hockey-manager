# Manager update: six connected improvements

The career broadcast now tracks individual two-minute penalties. Two penalties on one team produce five-on-three; penalties for both teams produce reduced equal-strength play. A third penalty waits until one of the first two clocks expires. Only the earliest active penalty on the disadvantaged team ends after a powerplay goal. Penalized players are excluded from all line changes. Overtime can reach five-on-three and returns to normal numbers at a stoppage after penalties expire. Hooking, tripping, holding, interference and slashing share the two-minute model. This is not a complete implementation of major, misconduct or coincidental-penalty regulations.

Recruitment needs include current availability, selected tactics, center familiarity, assessed junior alternatives, expiring contracts and signed future arrivals. Temporary gaps suggest loans; available academy alternatives suggest assessment before promotion. Roles can overlap, so counts must not be added into a squad total.

Detailed match reports keep their existing size limit. Before pruning, compact results and shot totals are preserved under Season & History. League player-statistics archives and recorded transfer history no longer expire after two seasons or 250 transfers. Existing season titles, tables and player summaries remain. Already deleted historical information is not reconstructed. Save imports accept up to 32 million characters, matching the compressed-save decoder limit.

Navigation and search-filter saves are coalesced over 700 ms and skip repeated whole-world normalization. Game actions retain synchronous saves; pending navigation state is flushed when the page is hidden. Browser back/forward preserves page, player, recruitment filter and scroll snapshots within the current career/session. History entries from another career cannot restore that career. Returning to a running fixture through navigation always leaves it paused.

Coaching follow-up supports 3, 5 or 10 matches for newly adopted focuses. Match exposure is counted once for equal strength, powerplay, boxplay and overtime, then displayed alongside rates per 60 minutes. Earlier reports without complete exposure remain usable for raw match averages but are excluded from normalized rates. Small samples and different opponents still affect comparisons.

Match settings offer pauses after goals, penalties, between periods and when opening statistics. Read-only statistics, ice time and event panels run live by default. Coach actions, required medical decisions, navigation away and backgrounding still pause. The paused view explains why. Settings are included in career saves and exports.

Validation: manager-six.test.cjs covers multiple/queued penalties, PP release, overtime, legacy penalty migration, settings, injury-aware recruitment, persistent histories, exposure and coalesced saving. browser-navigation.test.cjs exercises back/forward with a history API fixture, including player origins and career isolation. Existing match-rules, pause-recovery, coaching-cycle and workflow suites cover integration. The older clearance test now seeds the new penalty collection rather than mutating the obsolete singleton alone.
