# Player development

All players acquire a saved private developmentModel during saving or assessment. Migration preserves current attributes and training progress. Senior ceilings derive deterministically from remaining legacy attributeGrowth and individual attribute variation; academy ceilings retain their existing values, never below a current attribute. Profiles remain attached through transfers and promotion. Generated player attributes must be finalized before initializing their development profile.

Training, match development, academy development and AI training use developmentAdvance. Hidden individual pace, attribute-specific age curves and slower final steps determine progress. No growth exceeds a saved ceiling; progress is cleared at the ceiling. Existing participation, coaching, workload and injury gates remain in the callers. This unifies the growth calculation, not the amount of training exposure for every club. No calendar-day-only automatic growth is introduced.

Potential reports estimate role-weighted ceiling values with existing staff uncertainty; actual ceilings are not rendered. Own-player training panels show five recent attribute changes. Profiles retain at most 24 changes.

At preseason transition physical, technical and mental attributes decline on different curves with an individual timing offset. Goalkeeper physical peaks occur later. Fractional decline accumulates deterministically, attributes remain at least one, and each player ages once per season. Free agents and academy players use this same birthday path. Current training can offset decline subject to progressively slower age-based growth; ceiling values do not reroll or shrink. Decline is applied at the season transition, not on real birthdays or daily. Numerical curves are game-design choices, not medically or statistically calibrated predictions. Old saves have no reconstructable lifetime development history; tracking starts with this update.
